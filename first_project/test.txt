The Rise of Artificial Intelligence: Transforming Our World
Artificial Intelligence (AI) has rapidly transitioned from the realm of science fiction into a defining pillar of modern technology. Today, it touches almost every aspect of our daily lives, reshaping how we work, communicate, solve problems, and envision the future.

What is Artificial Intelligence?
At its core, AI refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines programmed to think, learn, adapt, and problem-solve. Unlike traditional software that follows rigid, pre-written rules, modern AI relies on Machine Learning (ML) and Deep Learning. These systems ingest vast amounts of data, identify patterns, and continuously improve their performance without human intervention.
